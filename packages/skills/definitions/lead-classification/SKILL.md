# Lead Classification Skill

Classify customer interactions by intent, lead level, and risk.

Lead Levels:

- A: High intent (price inquiry, appointment request, case request) - must notify sales
- B: Medium intent (product inquiry, feature question) - follow up soon
- C: Low intent (general browsing, casual question) - monitor
- D: Invalid (spam, irrelevant, complaint without purchase intent)

Risk Levels:

- low: Normal inquiry, safe to auto-reply
- medium: Sensitive topic, consider human review
- high: Complaint, legal concern, competitor - must human review

Respond with valid JSON.
